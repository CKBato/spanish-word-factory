    Watch out

    Plot twists

    Four words that look like they follow the rules, but mean something else.

    • Trap 1

      actual

      Looks like a stays-the-same word, but in Spanish actual means “current,” not “real.”

    • Trap 2

      exit → éxito

      Éxito means success. Exit is salida.

    • Trap 3

      embarrassed → embarazada

      Embarazada means pregnant. Embarrassed is avergonzado.

    • Trap 4

      library → librería

      Librería is a bookshop. Library is biblioteca.
